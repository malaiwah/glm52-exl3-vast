## FP8 KV comparison

A matched one-window, 2,047-position KLD evaluation used the same 3.36-bpw K6
weights and BF16 reference logits:

| KV record | mean KLD |
|---|---:|
| dynamic-token NVFP4 MLA + FP8 RoPE | `0.08250703559901781` |
| FP8 MLA + BF16 RoPE | `0.06866920795373557` |

FP8 improved mean KLD by about 16.8%, but it was not the production Pareto
winner. A 1,024-row prefill arena OOMed on the first 48 MiB Trellis temporary
at both 520,192 and 512,000 configured tokens. Reducing the arena and DCP
workspace to 512 made an exact 512,000-token pool boot and pass 5/5 needles in
a 509,010-token prompt, but left only about 81–93 MiB/GPU after the benchmark.

That safe FP8 shape measured 1,901.9 / 1,805.2 / 1,748.7 tok/s PP and
105.4 / 119.4 / 207.4 / 229.0 tok/s aggregate TG at C1/C2/C4/C8. Relative to
dynamic NVFP4, long PP fell about 18–21%, C8 fell 22.9%, and C8 MTP mean
acceptance length fell from 3.02 to 2.51. FP8 is therefore documented as a
quality experiment, not promoted as the turnkey default.
